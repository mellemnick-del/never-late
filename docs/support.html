How to play

Tap anywhere when the seconds hand is inside the glowing marker on the bezel. A normal hit scores 1. Hitting the bright center scores 2. If the hand passes the marker, or you tap outside it, the run ends.

Phases

  • Steady: the hand speeds up and the marker shrinks with every hit.
  • Reverse (8 hits): the hand can change direction after a hit.
  • Drift (15 hits): the marker slides along the bezel.

Common questions

No sound? Sound follows your ring/silent switch. Flip it to ring, and check the speaker button at the bottom left of the game.

Lost my best score? Your best score is stored on your device. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Paused? Leaving the app mid-run pauses the game. Tap to pick up where you left off.
